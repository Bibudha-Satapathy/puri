Welcome to Puri

Puri – The Spiritual and Coastal Gem of Odisha

Puri, nestled along the Bay of Bengal in Odisha, is a city that beautifully blends spirituality, culture, and natural charm.
Renowned worldwide for the majestic Jagannath Temple, it stands as one of the four sacred Char Dhams of Hindu
pilgrimage, drawing millions of devotees each year, especially during the grand Rath Yatra festival.
Beyond its religious significance, Puri offers visitors the allure of golden beaches, where the sunrise and sunset paint
breathtaking views, and the nearby Chilika Lake, a paradise for birdwatchers and nature lovers.
The city is also a hub of Odisha’s rich traditions, from Odissi dance and sand art to vibrant local markets and cuisine.
With its deep historical roots, spiritual aura, and coastal beauty, Puri is not just a destination but an experience that leaves
every traveler with a sense of peace and wonder.

Top Attractions in Puri

Jagannath Temple

One of the most revered Hindu temples, dedicated to Lord Jagannath. It is part of the Char Dham pilgrimage and famous for the annual Rath Yatra (Chariot Festival) that draws millions of devotees.

Puri Beach

A lively stretch along the Bay of Bengal, known for its golden sands, sunrise and sunset views, and vibrant atmosphere. It’s a favorite spot for both pilgrims and tourists to relax and enjoy the sea breeze.

Chilika Lake

Asia’s largest brackish water lagoon, located near Puri. It is a paradise for birdwatchers and nature lovers, with migratory birds in winter and playful dolphins near Satapada.

Konark Sun Temple

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, about 35 km from Puri. Famous for its stunning 13th‑century architecture shaped like a colossal chariot, it showcases Odisha’s rich heritage and artistry.
