Top Attractions in Puri

Jagannath Temple

One of the most revered Hindu temples, dedicated to Lord Jagannath. It is part of the Char Dham pilgrimage and famous for the annual Rath Yatra (Chariot Festival) that draws millions of devotees.

Puri Beach

A lively stretch along the Bay of Bengal, known for its golden sands, sunrise and sunset views, and vibrant atmosphere. It’s a favorite spot for both pilgrims and tourists to relax and enjoy the sea breeze.

Chilika Lake

Asia’s largest brackish water lagoon, located near Puri. It is a paradise for birdwatchers and nature lovers, with migratory birds in winter and playful dolphins near Satapada.

Konark Sun Temple

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, about 35 km from Puri. Famous for its stunning 13th‑century architecture shaped like a colossal chariot, it showcases Odisha’s rich heritage and artistry.
